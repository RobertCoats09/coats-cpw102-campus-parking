# Product Requirements

**Project:** Campus Parking Helper

**Team members:** Robert Coats

**Date:** September 30th 2026

## User and problem

Who is the user?
Students and visitors who park on campus

What problem does the user have?
They have to calculate parking costs themselves

## Goal

Our program will help the user: conveniently calculate their parking cost

## Requirements

Write three things the program must do. Make each one specific enough to test.

1. The program must accurately tell the user the parking cost it has estimated
2. The program must be operable from a computer terminal
3. The program must know the hourly cost for parking and the amount of time the user has parked for

## Not included

What will this version **not** do?

- assist in making a transaction

## Success

We will know the product works when:
We manually calculate the parking cost and compare with the program's output to verify
